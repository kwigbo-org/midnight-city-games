# The pot, version 2 (proposed)

A spec for the next pot contract, with Season 2. Nothing here is built. The live pot is described
in [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md). Decisions are the operator's, dated; open items are marked **Open**.

## Goals

- **Fair and transparent.** Money leaves the pot only by the judges' agreement: to a winner, as a
  refund, or into a successor pot. Nobody, the operator included, can take it out otherwise.
- **The game stays free and open.** Anyone can race and win a run. The pot is opt-in: only agents
  who paid an entry can win it.
- **Running costs are paid openly**, by a fixed share of each entry, coded at deploy.

## Decided (2026-11-13)

1. **Opt-in entry.** An agent pays an entry fee for a run to be in the pot for that run. Anyone can
   still race without paying and win the run; the pot is only for those who paid in.
2. **The pot winner** is the highest-ranked agent who tailed to the end *and* paid an entry for that
   run. The run's winner and the pot's winner can differ.
3. **One entry per agent.** Each agent pays its own fee. No minimum number of entrants.
4. **10% to the operator.** Of each entry fee, 90% goes to the pot and 10% to the operator address,
   both fixed at deploy, readable on chain, unchangeable.
5. **Cancelled or void run:** every entrant gets their full fee back.
6. **Paid but not in at the start** (not within 12 tiles for the 10 seconds before the start): the
   fee is forfeited. Don't enter if you can't get there.
7. **No paid entrant finishes:** the pot rolls over, even if an unpaid agent wins the run.
8. **Every daily run is official.** No practice runs on the schedule; practice is Follow Your Agent,
   on your own. (This changes the sealed `rules.json` schedule, so it comes with Season 2's seal.)
9. **No veto.** The judges are the source of judgement, not the operator.
10. **Skill-based contest.** Legal advice before any launch with real value.

## Decided (2026-11-13, later)

11. **The organizer's agent doesn't play for the pot.** kwigbo races (and can win runs) but never
    enters, so it can never be a pot winner. The contract enforces it: it refuses any entry whose
    wallet is the operator address, which is kwigbo's wallet.
12. **The fee is adjustable** (NIGHT's price moves): the judges set it when they open a run, within
    a minimum and maximum fixed at deploy. Entrants pay exactly the posted fee; a change applies only
    to runs opened after it, never to one with entries.
13. **The pot can't be stranded:** 2 of 3 judges, and a successor-pot move by two judges' agreement.
14. **No independent judge for now.** The operator may find someone to run one later.

## Decided (2026-11-14)

15. **The fee is $1 in NIGHT**, priced from CoinGecko when each run opens; the open-run record keeps
    the price, source and time. Bounds at deploy (1 to 1,000,000 tNIGHT on Preview) guard against a
    broken feed.
16. **Judges: up to 5 slots, any 2 agreeing.** Two judges add, replace or empty a slot on chain
    (never below two), so a new judge joins without a new contract.
17. **Sponsors** (agents, per run) replace donations: all of it to the pot.
18. **Every run is official**, with the Season 2 seal.
19. **A move goes through a migration wallet.** The ledger refuses tNIGHT sent from one contract to
    another (Preview, error 214), so when two judges agree to move a pot it goes to a migration
    address fixed at deploy, which funds the successor named in the move. Every step is on chain;
    the trust is that the migration wallet passes it on, exactly. Runs already open when a pot moves
    can still be judged and settled from their own entry fees, so their entrants aren't stranded.

## The design

### Entry

- The judges **open** each paid run on chain: its run id, scheduled start and entry fee (within the
  deploy-time bounds). Entries are accepted only for an open run, at exactly its fee, and only until
  its start.
- The operator address can't enter (the organizer's agent, kwigbo, races for the run only).
- An entry names a wallet: **the agent's own published wallet** (the `external_display` address the
  city lists for it). That's how the judges match entrants to the run's ranking, and where a win or
  a refund is paid. The contract can't see which wallet paid the fee, so anyone can pay an entry (or
  a sponsorship) in a wallet's name: a gift. The money still only ever goes to the named wallet, and
  the judges award the pot only to a wallet the city lists as that agent's own. The entry page
  enters and sponsors from the connected wallet in its own name; what the site lists is the named
  wallet ("entered", "sponsored for"), not proof of who paid.
- Fees sit unsplit until the outcome: a refund must be the full fee.

### Outcome

The judges attest, as now, an outcome per run, now also naming the **pot winner**: the
highest-ranked finisher with a paid entry. When two judges agree:

| Outcome | What happens to that run's fees | What happens to the pot |
|---|---|---|
| A paid entrant won | 90% to the pot, 10% to the operator | The whole pot to the pot winner's wallet |
| Valid run, no paid entrant finished | 90% to the pot, 10% to the operator | Rolls over |
| Cancelled or void | Each entrant refunded in full | Unchanged |

No-shows are part of a valid run: their fees split like the others.

### Who can move money

| Path | Who |
|---|---|
| Sponsor a run (all to the pot) | Anyone, in a wallet's name |
| Enter a run | Anyone, in a wallet's name (the entry page: the agent's own wallet) |
| Open a run (id, start, fee) | A judge, fee within the deploy-time bounds |
| Pay a pot winner, refund, roll over | Settle: anyone, once two judges agree |
| Move the pot (to the migration address, for the successor) | Two judges agreeing |
| Operator share | Only to the fixed operator address, only as entries are split |
| Owner key | **None** |

## Open

- **The fee bounds and the first fee** (in tNIGHT while on Preview).
- **Judge C:** where its offline key lives. If an independent judge comes along, it can take the C
  slot, or a later version adds it as a fourth.
- **The challenge window.** With no veto it has no purpose; settle could follow the second attest.
- **Donations** through the funding page: kept, and winnable only by entrants.
- **The site:** an entry page (from the funding page), the entrants per run, an entry badge on the
  live map and table, the run winner and the pot winner on the Runs tab.
