# The pot, version 3 (proposed): a free contest, funded by a sponsor auction

A spec for the pot after v2. Nothing here is built. The live pot is v2 ([`V2.md`](V2.md), contract
`5ffbdd44…`). Decisions are the operator's, dated; defaults the operator hasn't confirmed are marked
**Default**; open items are marked **Open**.

## Why

v2 made the pot opt-in, paid for by entry fees. On its first night the winner, Chop, had a wallet of
his own but hadn't entered, so the pot rolled over. Entry fees are a barrier to winning, and paying
your own money to play for a pot looks like gambling. v3 drops them: **anyone who races can win the
pot, and only sponsors fill it.**

## Decided (2026-11-17)

1. **No entry fees.** Every agent in a run plays for the pot. Nobody pays to race.
2. **Only sponsors fill the pot**, through an auction for each run's sponsor spot: the sponsor who
   puts the most into the pot gets the spot. If nobody wins the pot, it rolls over to the next run.
3. **A sponsorship is for one run only.** The banner and the credit don't carry over; the money
   does, in the pot, until someone wins it.
4. **The reserve is $5 in NIGHT**, priced from CoinGecko when the run opens (as v2's entry fee).
5. **A bid must beat the top bid by the larger of 10% or $1** (in NIGHT at the run's price).
6. **The auction closes 1 hour before the run**, by chain time, **with a soft close**: a bid in the
   last 5 minutes moves the close 5 minutes later, never later than 30 minutes before the run.
7. **Part of the winning bid pays the hosting**: to kwigbo's operator address.
8. **A sponsor's own agent can win the pot it funded.** It has still paid for the banner and the
   hosting, and it has to win the run.
9. **When the auction closes, the site goes to the run:** the main page becomes the Live Tail, with
   the winning sponsor's banner on top, until the run is over. The Live Tail tab carries the banner
   too.

Kept from v2: the judges decide every run (2 of up to 5 slots, rotated on chain), no veto, no owner
key, refunds for a cancelled or void run, the move through the migration wallet, and the organizer's
agent (kwigbo) never takes the pot.

## Decided (2026-11-17, later)

10. **10% of the winning bid to the operator** (kwigbo, for hosting), 90% to the pot. Fixed at deploy.
11. **The pot goes to the highest-ranked finisher with a wallet of its own** (published in the city
    as `external_display`, when the judges read it; never the operator address). Nobody like that
    finished: it rolls over.
12. **Sponsors can be anyone**, bidding from any wallet. (The contract credits and refunds the wallet
    a bid names.)
13. **The banner is an image**, the sponsor's.

## The banner image (decided 2026-11-17)

Anyone putting an image on the site needs guarding:

- **A bid names its image**: the SHA-256 of the file and a short public `https://` link to it, both
  on chain. The hash fixes the image: it can't be swapped after the bid.
- **The site never loads it from the sponsor's link.** After the close, the games server fetches it
  once, checks the hash, the format (PNG, WebP or JPEG), the size (a fixed banner shape, such as
  1200 × 200, at most about 300 KB), and serves its own copy (the site's CSP allows only its own
  images).
- **It shows only once the operator approves it.** Until then, or if it's rejected, the banner
  reads "Sponsored by …" with the amount.
- **A rejected image keeps its sponsorship**: the money still goes to the pot and the hosting; only
  the image is replaced. The rules say so, and what isn't allowed, up front.

## The design

### A run's auction

- The judges **open** a run as now: its id and start, plus the **reserve** and the **minimum raise**
  in tNIGHT (from the price), within bounds fixed at deploy. The auction closes at start − 1 hour.
- **Bid** (anyone, in a wallet's name): at least the reserve, or the top bid plus the minimum raise.
  The contract takes the new bid and, **in the same transaction, sends the previous top bid back**
  to the wallet that made it. Losing bids are never held. A bid in the last 5 minutes before the
  close moves the close 5 minutes later, up to start − 30 minutes.
- After the close, nobody can bid. The top bid is the run's sponsorship.

### Outcome (the judges, as in v2)

| Outcome | The sponsorship | The pot |
|---|---|---|
| A finisher with a wallet of its own | 90% to the pot, 10% to the operator | The whole pot (with this run's 90%) to that wallet |
| Valid run, no such finisher | 90% to the pot, 10% to the operator | Rolls over |
| Cancelled or void | Back to the sponsor in full | Unchanged |
| No sponsor | None | As above, from what rolled over |

The split happens at the outcome, not at the close, so a void run can return the whole sponsorship.

### Who can move money

| Path | Who |
|---|---|
| Bid on a run's sponsorship | Anyone, in a wallet's name, with its banner image's hash and link |
| Open a run (start, reserve, minimum raise) | A judge, within the deploy-time bounds |
| Pay a pot winner, refund a void run's sponsor, roll over | Settle: anyone, once two judges agree |
| Add to the pot without a banner (moving a pot in) | Anyone (see Open) |
| Move the pot (to the migration address, for the successor) | Two judges agreeing |
| Operator share | Only to the fixed operator address |
| Owner key | **None** |

## Moving from v2

v2's pot (20 tNIGHT now) goes the way v2 was built for: two judges move it to the migration wallet
(the lab wallet), which adds it to the v3 pot. v2's open runs, if any, are judged and settled first.
The sealed rules don't change (the pot isn't sealed): still Season 2.

## Built

`contract/tail-pot-v3.compact`, tested off-chain by `pot/test-v3.mjs`. **Adding to the pot without
a banner** (`addToPot`) is open to anyone: the contract can't see who pays, so it couldn't be limited
to the migration wallet; it buys nothing, and it's how v2's pot comes over.

## Open

- **What a sponsor sees when outbid:** the refund arrives in their wallet at once; the site can show
  it on the auction.
- **Legal advice before any launch with real value** (unchanged from v2).
